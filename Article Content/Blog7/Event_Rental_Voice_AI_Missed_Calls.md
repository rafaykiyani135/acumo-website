From the first ring to a deposit-paid booking, four steps.

1. **Inbound call connects.** The AI answers in under half a second.
2. **The AI qualifies the job.** It captures event date, guest count, surface type, and power requirements to determine viable inventory.
3. **Real-time calendar validation.** The agent confirms availability for the tent, inflatable, or decor package before promising anything.
4. **Instant SMS with a deposit link.** The caller gets a dynamic quote and a one-tap deposit link while event data writes to the CRM.
