01 — Mark

Logo & Wordmark

The Acumo mark is a nested, continuous-line "A" built from circuit-like strokes and nodes — a literal reference to connected systems and intelligent infrastructure.

Primary / on dark
ACUMO AI Wordmark / on brand blue

Usage: Keep the mark on dark backgrounds or Acumo Blue. Preserve clear space equal to the height of the inner "A" on all sides. Never stretch, recolor outside the palette, add outlines, or place the white mark on light backgrounds without a dark container.

06 — Voice & Motion

How We Sound & Move

Acumo speaks like a senior engineer: direct, precise, never hype-driven. Motion is slow-in, fast-out, and always purposeful.

Voice Principles

  • Precise, not loud. Say the concrete thing. Avoid superlatives and AI buzzwords.
  • Engineer to engineer. Respect the reader's intelligence. Explain architecture, not vibes.
  • Editorial rhythm. Short sentences. Land the point. Use the serif for warmth, never for filler.
  • Confident, not arrogant. Show outcomes. Let the work carry the claim.

Layout Rules

  • Grid: 12-column, 24px gutter, max width 1400px, 40px side padding.
  • Radius: pills (999px) for actions and tags; 12–16px for cards and panels. No sharp cards.
  • Borders: 1px hairlines via rgba(255,255,255,.05–.15). Structure comes from lines, not shadows.
  • Spacing: section padding 80–160px; content gaps in 8px increments.
